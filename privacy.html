Privacy Policy

Cats Explained Publisher · Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Summary

Cats Explained Publisher is a private tool that lets the channel owner publish their own videos to their own TikTok account. It collects only what TikTok needs to make that work, and does not sell or share data.

2. Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar), to confirm which account is connected.
  • An access token, used only to upload and publish videos you choose.

We do not access your followers, messages, or other people's data.

3. How data is stored

Access tokens are stored securely by our integration provider, Composio, and used only to perform the uploads you request. Videos are sent directly to TikTok and are not kept by the app afterwards.

4. Sharing

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone, except TikTok (to publish your videos) and Composio (to hold the connection securely).

5. Your choices

You can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. After disconnecting, the stored access token stops working and can be deleted on request.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent through the Cats Explained YouTube channel.
